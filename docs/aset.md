## Dapur

Berkas: `assets/dapur.png`.

Prompt:

> Create a photorealistic editorial home photograph for a practical Indonesian home cleaning guide. Landscape 3:2 composition. A modest but beautiful contemporary Indonesian kitchen, natural soft daylight through a window, pale cool grey ceramic wall and counter, pale oak cabinet fronts, brushed stainless steel sink and simple faucet, one small green plant. In foreground at lower right, a neatly folded muted forest green microfiber cloth and plain unbranded small translucent pump bottle. Absolutely no people, no text, no logos, no labels, no graphics, no overlay. Realistic lived-in attainable home, clean and dry, tactile real materials, restrained colors, soft shadows, architectural magazine photo without luxury excess. Composition should feature counter and sink on right with calm natural negative space on left. This is an illustrative lifestyle photograph, not evidence of a cleaning result. Output one image.

Prompt:

> Photorealistic editorial lifestyle photograph for an Indonesian household cleaning website. Portrait 4:5 framing. Close view of a modest contemporary bathroom with light cool-gray ceramic tiles, brushed chrome shower fittings, a narrow glass partition, daylight, a simple small forest green rubber squeegee resting neatly on a low light-gray tiled shelf, and one neatly folded green microfiber cloth. No people, no text, no lettering, no logos, no before/after comparison. Composition appropriate for a supporting section about preventing water deposits by drying surfaces. Output one photograph.
